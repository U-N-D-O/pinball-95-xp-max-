## Working title

## Short description

## Full description

Launch the ball, work the flippers, and chase a new high score in a colorful dog-park pinball table built for portrait play.

Hit paw-print bumpers, tennis-ball targets, chew toys, ramps, the fire hydrant spinner, and the water-bowl bonus. Complete the paw mission to open the doghouse and keep your three-ball run alive with the water save.

Pinball Neo 95 combines chunky pixel art and crisp HD scaling with touch controls, optional haptic feedback, arcade sound effects, pause-and-resume safety, and local high-score saving.
